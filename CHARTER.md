# Northstar Pod Team Working Agreement & Charter

## 1. Team Purpose & Scope
This pod is commissioned for 1 week to build a Support Deflection MVP for Northstar Retail Co., aiming to reduce manual ticket handling across Order Status, Returns & Refunds, and Stock Availability inquiries.

## 2. Communication Norms & Schedules
- **Daily Standup**: Every morning at 10:00 AM (15 mins) to review board movements, identify blockers, and confirm task ownership.
- **Async Updates**: End-of-day channel post at 5:00 PM summarizing completed tasks and planned work for the next day.
- **Primary Channel**: Dedicated Team Chat / Slack / Discord.

## 3. Project Board & Anti-Black-Box Rules
- **Task Granularity**: No task on the board may exceed 4 hours of estimated work. Any task estimated > 4 hrs must be split before work begins.
- **Definition of Done (DoD)**: Every card must have a clear, 1-sentence DoD that can be verified in a single inspection step.
- **Same-Day Updates**: Cards MUST be moved on the board on the exact day work is started/completed. End-of-week batch updates are strictly forbidden to ensure audit log integrity.

## 4. Git Commit & Audit Trail Conventions
- **Commit Format**: `<type>: <what changed> - <why it matters>`
  - Approved types: `feat`, `fix`, `docs`, `style`, `refactor`, `test`, `data`.
  - Non-compliant messages (e.g., "wip", "updates", "fixes") will require commit squashing or re-documentation.
- **Branch Naming**: `feature/<task-id>-<short-description>`

## 5. Conflict Resolution & Escalation Path
- **Level 1**: Direct peer-to-peer 1-on-1 dialogue within 12 hours of friction.
- **Level 2**: Team discussion during morning standup to reallocate resources or reassign tasks.
- **Level 3 (Zero-Activity Rule)**: If any pod member shows **zero visible activity for 2 consecutive days** (0 commits, 0 board updates), the issue is escalated immediately to sprint facilitators per Northstar Procurement rules.
